McCUp

Onion eating. Human bowling. Snowman relays. McGill's newest tradition is a night of very silly challenges and one very big bonfire.

Sign up your team See the challenges

Bringing campus life back Lower Field 2026. Be there.

One day, one field, every faculty. McCup takes the spirit of McGill's weirdest competitions and turns it into a proper campus-wide event.

Teams of 4 to 6 students go head to head in a run of ridiculous challenges. Everyone else posts up in the chill zone with food and drinks to cheer, heckle, and keep score.

  • 👥
    Mix your facultiesTeams must include students from at least 2 different faculties to compete for top spots.
  • 📊
    Live leaderboardReal-time scoring displayed after every challenge. Compete for placement or just for bragging rights.
  • 🏁
    Spirit pointsJudges award points for team spirit, creative costumes, and the loudest fan support.
  • 🍖
    Chill zoneSpectators and non-competing teams enjoy food trucks, hot drinks, music, and prime viewing areas.
  • 🔥
    Bonfire finaleWinning teams crowned at sunset. Everyone gathers around the fire to celebrate with food and music.
